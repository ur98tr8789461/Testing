A little about me

Hi, I’m Sean.
Glad you’re here.

I’m a photographer drawn to the in-between: the unplanned laugh, the long way home, the light that only lasts a minute. I make thoughtful images for good people and meaningful places.

When I’m not behind the camera, you’ll find me wandering a trail, hunting down the best coffee in town, or taking the scenic route just because.
